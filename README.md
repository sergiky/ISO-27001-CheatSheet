# ISO-27001-CheatSheet

We are working at RandomCorp

# Foundations

- ISO 27001 is a management system standard - how you decide and prove, not what you bought
- Clauses 4-10 are the requirements; Annex A is the control set you justify against your list
- Every concept lands on RandomCorp, so you see the decision and not just the definition

- ISO 27001 provides REQUIREMENTS for an ISMS - not guidelines on how to implement one
- 'Shall' is obligatory and auditable; 'should' is guidance. 27001 shalls, 27002 shoulds
- You certify against 27001 only - ISO 27002 certification does not exist

## Clauses 4-10 and Annex A
- Unnumbered introduction, then clauses 1-3 (scope, vocabulary), then clauses 4-10, then Annex A
- Clauses 4-10 are auditable and cannot be excluded - scope and control applicability are what your decided
- Annex A: 93 controls in 4 themes - Organizational 37, People 8, Physical 14, Technological 34
